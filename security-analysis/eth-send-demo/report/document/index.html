Executive Summary

Condensed findings for decision-makers. Detail and evidence follow in Sections 02–04.

The subject address is a high-value, accumulation-type wallet on the TRON network holding approximately $626.6k USD, 99.8% of which is concentrated in genuine Tether USDT on the canonical Tether contract. The wallet exhibits an inflow-only stablecoin profile — no outbound TRC-20 transfer appears anywhere in the sampled ledger window — alongside intensive owner-side resource management via repeated Energy delegation cycles.

Findings & Flag Register

Each finding cites on-chain evidence observable at report block height. Severity reflects impact on the holder, not attribution of wrongdoing.

F-01
Balance-mimicking counterfeit token injection high
Three inbound transfers of a token labelled “TG: jieuu”, each carrying exactly 625,214.92 units — deliberately matching the wallet's genuine USDT balance to within 0.01%. The symbol embeds a Telegram contact handle. This is a precision-targeted fake-balance / phishing lure: the holder is meant to see a plausible “second USDT” position and contact the embedded handle.
Evidence: contract interaction events ×3 · sender TUZPztRs…Gjieuu · injected amount vs. genuine balance deviation <0.005%
F-02
Sustained dusting campaign — 30+ fragmented micro-senders medium
More than thirty distinct addresses deposited genuine USDT in the $0.05–$30 band. Individually immaterial (~$156 total); collectively a classic address-poisoning surface: the history is flooded so that a lookalike sender address can later be confused with a legitimate counterparty at send-time.
Evidence: 39 distinct sending addresses in window · median inflow $1–10 · zero reciprocal outbound flow
F-03
Counterfeit / spam token cluster medium
Eight non-canonical token contracts delivered to the address, including typo-squatted “UDST”, “FUSDTM”, zero-value “USD1”, a $78,788-denominated “USD” impostor, casino-ad tokens (“ha138 com”, “ip292”) and “UNBANME” phishing bait. All are unsolicited airdrops whose purpose is address-book poisoning or advertising.
Evidence: 8 distinct non-canonical contracts · all inbound-only · none on Chainscaner canonical list
F-04
Inflow-only stablecoin profile observe
No outbound TRC-20 movement exists in the sampled history despite a $625k position. Combined with the delegation cycles this indicates deliberate fund immobilisation — a treasury posture that also means any future outbound transfer would constitute a first-of-kind event for this address.
Evidence: 50-record TRC-20 window · 0 outbound · 31 resource-management ops by owner
F-05
Concentrated single-issuer exposure observe
99.78% of assessed value rests in one instrument — genuine Tether USDT on TRON — carrying issuer-level freeze risk. Tether can freeze addresses at contract level; a flagged address becomes illiquid regardless of holder intent.
Evidence: holding distribution table, Section 01
Assessment: the wallet shows the exposure profile of a target, not an operator — every adverse artefact is inbound and unsolicited. No outbound flow to sanctioned entities, mixers or drainers was observed. The elevated score reflects how aggressively the address is being worked by poisoning actors, which is itself material risk: it signals the holder's balance is publicly known and contested.
