FAQ

Is scanning safe for my wallet?

Yes. A scan only reads public ledger state — the same data any block explorer shows. No signature, approval or transaction is requested during a lookup.

What does a "flagged" result mean?

The address or a token contract it holds appears in our review database. A flag is a risk indicator from operator review — always verify counterparties independently before interacting.

Why is an address "not found"?

TRON accounts only exist on-chain after their first received transaction. A valid-format address with no history simply hasn't been activated yet.
